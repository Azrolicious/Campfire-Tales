# Campfire Tales roadmap

Ideas waiting their turn. Not part of the addon itself.

## Ideas

- **Ork and troll in the tooltips.** Point at a button and see the line as Krump or Juju will say it, before it goes into the chat line. Needs Krump and Juju to share their translator through `RoleplayAccents`, which is a small change to both.
- **Taking turns.** Players at the same fire who all have Campfire Tales could pass a "talking stick" round, so two stories never get told at once. Would use addon messages to the group.
- **Told already.** Grey out the stories you have told to the people at this fire, so nobody hears the Last Ember twice in one night.
- **Campfire items.** Say something about what was put in the fire, once it is clear how Forever names them.
